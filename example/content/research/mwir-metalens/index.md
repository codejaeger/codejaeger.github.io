## Abstract

We present a computational imaging approach for capturing thermal and long-range infrared scenes across the 3–5 micrometer mid-wave infrared (MWIR) spectrum. Rather than engineering complex metalens designs to handle multiple wavelengths simultaneously, we leverage a straightforward all-silicon hyperboloid metalens paired with machine learning reconstruction. The method requires minimal training data — just four image samples — to recover high-quality broadband imagery despite the optical system's wavelength-dependent characteristics.

## Key Contributions

- **All-Silicon Hyperboloid Metalens**: A compact MWIR metalens design exploiting hyperboloid geometry for broadband focusing across the 3–5 μm band.
- **Data-Efficient ML Reconstruction**: A learning-based reconstruction method requiring only four training samples to compensate for chromatic aberrations and recover broadband imagery.
- **Broadband MWIR Imaging**: Demonstrated high-quality thermal imaging across the full MWIR spectrum without dispersion-compensation engineering.
- **Compact System Design**: Eliminates the need for intricate multi-layer optics, enabling lightweight infrared imaging systems.
